Common questions

How do I sync between devices? Sign in to the same iCloud account on each device and keep iCloud turned on for Haloscript in Settings. Changes can take a short while to appear.

I deleted something by mistake. Open Home, tap the … menu, then Recently Deleted. Items stay for 30 days.

How do I back up my novel? Home → … → Back Up & Restore. Export to Word, ePub, Markdown or text from inside a novel.

How do I bring in my writing? Home → … → Import Manuscript (Word, Markdown or text).
