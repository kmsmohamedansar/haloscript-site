Haloscript Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Haloscript does not collect, store or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no developer-run servers.

Your writing

Your novels, notes, attachments and whiteboards are stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, they sync between your own devices through your private iCloud (CloudKit) storage. That data is held by Apple under your Apple ID and is not accessible to the developer.

Research browser

The research panel includes a small web browser. Pages you open there are loaded from the websites you choose and are handled by those sites under their own policies. Haloscript does not record or send your browsing anywhere.

Files you add

Images, PDFs and manuscripts you import are copied into the app on your device only.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.
